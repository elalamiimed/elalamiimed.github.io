01 — About

Curious about markets, fluent in code.

I'm a Moroccan computer science student at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, working where markets meet software.

I research systematic trading — forex, gold, equities, ETFs and crypto — studying how price actually behaves, then building the tooling to test it: entry and exit logic, risk rules, backtesting workflows. Python is my daily driver; I use TypeScript for agent software and Java/SQL for the systems side of my degree.

In October 2024 I founded HITSZCS, an open library of notes, past papers and study material for HITSZ computer-science students. It now has 53 stars, 9 forks and a contributor team of 16, and lives at Hitszcs.com.

I'm Director of General Affairs at HSIYC, HITSZ's first formally recognised international student organisation — 167 members across five departments. I lead a 23-person department, own club administration and recruitment (127 applicants scored), and author the governance and progress decks. I also organise Hack Hive, a 71-hour student hackathon, and built its event site and team-matching backend.

Before HITSZ I studied Chinese at Qingdao Agricultural University, graduating with a merit certificate. I'm now looking for a quantitative research or trading internship.
